# Project Constitution

This is the human-readable explanation of `project-constitution.v1.json`.

## Constitutional principles

1. The North Star remains visible above every task.
2. AI has broad technical authority inside approved Scope.
3. The owner controls goals, priorities, costs, protected actions, Merge, Release, and Deployment.
4. Evidence determines factual reality.
5. Scope changes are explicit and versioned.
6. Implementation, validation, Merge, and current-main verification are distinct.
7. Major methods pass Decision Intelligence.
8. Critical owner views are generated from canonical state.
9. Dogfooding observations require explicit promotion authority.
10. The personal-project security profile retains a mandatory safety floor.
