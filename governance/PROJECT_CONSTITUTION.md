# Project Constitution

This document is the human-readable projection of `project-constitution.v1.json`.

## Constitutional principles

1. **North Star permanence** — the complete objective remains visible above every task.
2. **AI technical authority** — AI researches, compares, designs, implements, validates, and repairs inside approved scope.
3. **Owner authority** — the owner controls goals, priorities, scope approval, costs, protected actions, Merge, Release, and Deployment.
4. **Evidence reality** — execution and completion claims require evidence.
5. **Scope truth** — exclusions, deferrals, and changes are explicit and versioned.
6. **Progress truth** — implementation, validation, Merge, and post-Merge verification are distinct.
7. **Decision Intelligence** — major methods are selected through research and weighted comparison, not first-idea bias.
8. **AI operability** — selected methods must be practical for an AI technical operator and a non-technical owner.
9. **Dual-audience rendering** — owner communication is simple; agent contracts are rigorous; both derive from one state.
10. **Dogfooding with promotion control** — observed lessons remain candidates until validated and explicitly promoted.
11. **Low-security personal profile** — minimize unnecessary controls while preserving the mandatory safety floor.
12. **No silent self-modification** — no model, validator, or observer may silently rewrite canonical authority.
