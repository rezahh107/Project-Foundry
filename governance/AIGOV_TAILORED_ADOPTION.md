## Adopted spirit

Project Foundry adopts these principles:

- AI is the technical decision authority inside approved scope.
- Evidence is the authority for factual reality.
- Owner action is not automatically technical approval.
- Scope and Progress are separate and explicit.
- Tool execution claims require invocation and result evidence.
- Owner reporting is the smallest complete truth plus one precise next action.
- Personal repositories may use owner-controlled Merge and minimal review.
- The mandatory safety floor remains active for sensitive boundaries.

## Tailoring

This repository does not claim full implementation of every AIGOV rule. The current status is `tailored_candidate_adoption`. A rule is not reported as enforced unless a repository carrier, validator, fixture, and supporting evidence exist.

## Initial enforced subset

The first validator-backed subset covers:

- North Star identity consistency;
- active Scope membership;
- lifecycle separation;
- completion evidence requirements;
- Decision Intelligence records for major decisions;
- no automatic dogfooding promotion;
- canonical state consistency.
